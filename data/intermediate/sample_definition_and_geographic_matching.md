# Sample definition and geographic matching

## Analysis sample

The study uses all records assigned to the official folklore category in the national representative intangible cultural heritage lists. The source snapshot contains 492 unique folklore records.

- **492 records** are retained as the national folklore sample.
- **11 records** are attributed to national or central-level units without an assignable provincial location. They are retained in the national sample and excluded from provincial mapping and analysis.
- **481 records** have a regional location and are available for regional mapping and counts. This total includes 6 Hong Kong records and 4 Macao records.
- **471 records** are assigned to the 31 mainland provincial-level units and enter the population-standardized, spatial, and regression analyses.
- **31 provincial-level units** are the observations in the provincial analysis panel.

The sample flow is 492 − 11 = 481, followed by 481 − 6 − 4 = 471.

## Record-level audit

`record_inclusion_geocoding_audit.csv` records the inclusion decision and geographic matching for each of the 492 records. `data/processed/provincial_analysis_panel_2020.csv` provides the 31-unit analysis panel. The source-linked record table and variable definitions are kept alongside the analysis data.
